Who Vash Admin is for

Vash Admin is a digital system for venues, event organizers and teams responsible for audiences, ticketing, content and sales.

Designed for:

  • Theatres and philharmonic venues
  • Concert halls, cultural centres, clubs and other event venues
  • Event organizers, producers and touring companies
  • Venues with their own ticketing systems or external ticketing partners
  • Sports facilities and event spaces
  • Restaurants and hospitality businesses managing table and seat reservations
