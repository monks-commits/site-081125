Compensatory Admission After an Air-Raid Alert

If an air-raid alert interrupts a performance after it has begun, a previously scanned ticket may be reactivated for one compensatory admission to another performance. If that performance is also interrupted after it has begun, the same ticket may be activated again.

An air-raid alert interrupts the performance

Some tickets have already been scanned at the entrance. VA Recovery allows such a ticket to be reactivated, used once at another performance and its use recorded in the system.

Each activation grants the right to one compensatory admission only. If the performance at which that right was redeemed is also interrupted after it begins, the same ticket may be reactivated for another one-time admission. This process can be repeated as often as necessary until the event has been completed in full.

Activating the compensation entitlement

A previously scanned ticket is reactivated. The activated ticket entitles its holder to one compensatory admission to another performance: either the same seat or an available alternative, at the administration's discretion.

Once redeemed, that compensation entitlement is considered used and cannot grant admission again. However, if the performance where the compensatory admission took place was also interrupted after starting, a new entitlement may be created for the same ticket.

Admission Control

Recovery Scanner checks the activated compensation entitlement and immediately displays the admission result. Each activated entitlement can only be redeemed once.

The “already used” status means that this particular compensation cycle is closed. It does not prevent a new activation of the same ticket if the performance attended under that entitlement is interrupted again after starting.

Successive compensation cycles

The same ticket can have multiple successive compensation cycles. Each is created separately, has its own status and permits just one admission.

For example: a ticket is scanned at the first performance, which is then interrupted — the first compensation entitlement is created. The spectator uses it to attend a second performance. If the second performance is also interrupted, the system can create a second compensation entitlement for the same ticket after the first one has been redeemed.

The chain may be repeated as often as necessary until the event is completed in full, after which the ticket's outstanding compensation obligation is considered closed.

Redemption records

Each activation and each redemption is recorded separately. The system shows which interrupted performance gave rise to the entitlement, at which performance it was redeemed and whether a new open entitlement remains after another interruption.

Thus, even if the same ticket receives compensation repeatedly, a complete history is preserved: the origin of each entitlement, where it was used and when the entire compensation chain was finally closed.
