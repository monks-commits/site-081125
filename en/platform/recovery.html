VA Recovery — Compensation Admission

When a performance is interrupted after starting, a ticket already scanned at the entrance may be reactivated for a single compensatory admission to another performance.

Interrupted performance

An interruption can leave visitors holding valid tickets that have already been checked at the first entrance. Recovery records a new, controlled admission right without deleting the original ticket history.

Activation is not admission

Activating a previously used ticket grants one compensatory admission. The venue may allocate the original seat or an available replacement seat under its own rules.

The Recovery scanner

At the entrance, the scanner checks the currently active compensation right. A successful scan consumes that right. A second scan in the same compensation cycle is rejected as already used.

Repeated interruptions

If the replacement performance is also interrupted after it begins, another one-time compensation right may be created for the same original ticket. Each cycle remains separate.

Audit trail

The system records the source interrupted performance, activation, the performance where compensation was used, and the final closure of each cycle.
