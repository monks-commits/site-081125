VA Gateway

Connecting external sales channels and independent systems while keeping the venue in control of its own ticketing operations.

VA Gateway is an integration tool, not a replacement for the venue's systems

A venue can retain its operating model, connect external operators and partner channels, and keep its own processes, rules and ticketing environment.

Additional sales channels The venue can connect external operators and partner systems to selected events.
The venue remains in control The venue decides which events, halls and functions an external channel may access.
No need to rebuild existing workflows The Gateway complements the current digital environment rather than forcing a fresh start.

How a connection through VA Gateway works

We show the practical result publicly, rather than the technical implementation: a venue can work with different independent sales channels through a coordinated integration.

External operator An independent sales channel with its own storefront and working environment.
Partner channel Event sales through a separate partner resource or a specialized channel.
VA Gateway Coordinated interaction between the venue system and external channels without exposing the internal architecture.
Venue system The venue's own ticketing environment, workstations, sales, admission control and internal processes.
In-house ticket sales The venue's online storefront, box office and other internal channels continue operating as usual.

What the venue gains

The value of VA Gateway lies not in technical detail but in the practical opportunities it opens up for the venue.

Operational flexibility

The venue can shape its own sales model: operate entirely in-house or add external channels when they are genuinely needed.

Managed connections

External capabilities are connected in an orderly way, according to the venue's real needs, sales policy and event programme.

Preservation of the existing environment

The venue keeps its systems, workstations and internal processes when opening additional channels for cooperation.

Ways to use VA Gateway

Connections are configured according to each venue's operating model, not a one-size-fits-all template.

Connecting external operators

If a venue wants to continue or expand cooperation with outside ticketing operators, VA Gateway supports this without requiring a complete rebuild of its own system.

Connecting independent systems

If a venue already operates its own digital infrastructure, individual parts of its ecosystem can interact through VA Gateway as a dedicated coordination layer.

The internal architecture and operating principles of VA Gateway are not disclosed publicly and constitute commercially sensitive information.

Practical Gateway demonstrations are offered during presentations to prospective clients, using scenarios closely aligned with their actual sales model and work with external operators.
