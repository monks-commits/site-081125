VA Gateway — External Ticketing Channels

Connect external sales channels and independent systems while the venue retains control over its own ticketing operations.

Integration instead of replacement

The gateway is designed as a coordination layer. A venue can continue using its own rules, workplaces and sales processes while connecting selected partners.

Venues control access

The venue decides which events, halls, seat inventory and permitted operations are available to an external channel. Connecting a partner does not transfer management of the venue’s own system.

A consistent ticketing state

Independent sales environments can exchange approved information through the gateway instead of managing competing copies of the hall independently.

Flexible commercial deployment

A venue may sell through its own website and box office, add partner channels for selected events, or keep separate seat allocations while a deeper integration is being agreed.

What the demo shows

The public demonstration focuses on the practical result: coordinated availability, permitted partner operations and transparent control of the hall. Production integrations depend on agreements with each external system.
