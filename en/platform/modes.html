Operating Modes

Choose the mode that matches a venue’s requirements: online, autonomous or hybrid.

Offline / autonomous

Local ticket sales and admission can continue without an Internet connection. This mode is useful where box-office sales and resilience are important.

Online

Customers purchase through the website using an interactive hall plan. The venue can run its own sales and, where supported by agreement, work alongside external operators.

Hybrid

Combine box-office and online workflows, using defined allocations or agreed synchronization arrangements to keep event inventory under control.
