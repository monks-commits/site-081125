Operating Modes

The system can operate autonomously, online or in a hybrid format. Each venue chooses a mode according to its needs. The system provides its own sales-management tools — independently or synchronized with existing partners, at the venue's discretion.

Offline Mode (Autonomous Box Office)

Fully independent operation without an Internet connection. Tickets are sold locally. Suitable for venues that sell most of their tickets at the box office.

Watch video

Online Mode

Online ticket sales through a website with an interactive seating plan.

The platform can operate independently or in parallel with existing ticketing partners.

Watch video

Hybrid Mode

A combination of box-office and online sales with subsequent data synchronization. An optimal choice for venues using a mixed sales model.
