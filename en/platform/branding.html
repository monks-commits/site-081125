01 · BRAND

The venue's brand — its name, reputation and mark of quality

Spectators buy more than a seat. They place their trust in a theatre, philharmonic hall, concert venue or other organization — its name, reputation and experience.

In its own digital environment, the venue's brand should remain at the forefront: from the storefront and event page to the seating plan, e-ticket and communication with spectators.

  • custom logo and visual identity;
  • branded storefront and event pages;
  • seating-plan design in the venue's style;
  • branded electronic tickets;
  • White Label for the venue's own digital environment.
A ticket is more than a QR-code carrier. It is part of the venue's brand.
One Demo 1 storefront, one shared venue brand — yet a distinct visual personality for every event.
02 · DIGITAL SCENOGRAPHY

Digital scenery for an individual event

The venue brand stays consistent, while each play, concert or other event can acquire its own digital identity.

The poster, Live Poster, video background and seating-plan design can work as digital stage scenery — extending the artistic language of a particular event into the moment of choosing and buying tickets.

VASH ADMIN uses prepared media assets and incorporates them into the event's digital journey. The system is not intended to replace video editors, poster design tools or other creative-content applications.

One physical hall can take on different digital scenographic designs for different events.
One hall. Three events. Three different digital stage designs.
02.1 · SOUND SCENOGRAPHY

Music design and voice-over

The event's visual identity can be enriched by short musical phrases, sound accents or spoken narration.

Just one or two musical phrases can instantly set the mood, trigger an association and prepare the spectator emotionally before they choose a seat.

Music or a voice can accompany a Live Poster, a short video poster or a transition into the seating plan, making the digital journey an audiovisual part of the event.

Sometimes a few seconds of a familiar melody are enough to bring an entire story to mind.

03 · IMMERSION

The event begins before entering the hall

In VA, immersion is not a separate effect or decoration. It means continuity of experience as the spectator moves from first discovering an event to purchasing a ticket without leaving its visual and emotional world.

Buying a ticket becomes part of the event experience rather than a technical interruption between the poster and the hall.

The venue's brand remains recognizable. The event has a distinct identity. The spectator completes the journey without a visual break.
No visual disruption

The poster, Live Poster, sound, seating plan and ticket can all form parts of one artistic narrative. Even when choosing a seat and paying, the spectator stays within the atmosphere of the event.

04 · ADDITIONAL SERVICE

Event design and content preparation

Where needed, the VASH ADMIN team can do more than integrate ready-made materials into the system: it can also help create a unified digital identity for a particular event.

poster and event visual design;
Live Poster and short video clips;
static and moving backgrounds for the seating plan;
musical design, sound accents and voice-over;
adapting assets for the storefront, ticket and digital channels;
selecting visual and audio solutions that suit the nature of the event.

We maintain extensive working archives of video and audio materials and use modern digital technologies to create, adapt, voice and visually design content. This enables a cohesive solution to be developed quickly for a particular play, concert or other event.

The aim is not merely to decorate a sales page, but to create a recognizable event atmosphere before the visitor even enters the hall.

The possibilities for designing different types of events are limited primarily by the venue team's imagination and the event's creative vision.

Brand builds trust. Digital scenography makes each event distinctive. Immersion turns the spectator's digital journey into part of the event itself.
