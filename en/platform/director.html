VASH ADMIN · PLATFORM

Management Dashboard

A single management view of events, sales, reservations, venue occupancy, actual admissions and results by sales channel.

A management layer, not another cashier screen

The dashboard brings together results from working modules and presents them at the level needed by the venue director.

Event lifecycle

Separate upcoming, current, completed, postponed, cancelled and archived events. The relevant indicators change with each stage.

Before the performance

Monitor issued inventory, tickets sold, reservations, available seats, ticketing channels, presale pace and hall occupancy.

During the performance

Review actual scanned admissions, attendance percentage, ticket utilization and current admission status.

After the performance

Compare sales totals, attendance, distribution channels and the final event result. Retain history for later analysis.

Operational oversight

Access is separated by role: management observes the overall picture, while departments work in their assigned functional areas.
