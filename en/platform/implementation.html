Implementation

VASH ADMIN technologies are deployed in stages, taking into account the specific requirements of each venue.

Every theatre, concert hall, cinema or other venue has its own seating layout, sales model and internal procedures. The system is therefore configured individually.

1. Initial assessment

We establish the hall configuration, seating capacity, section layout and current sales model. Financial reporting requirements and the option to work alongside existing partners are considered separately.

2. Creating the digital seating plan

An individual digital seating plan is created, including sections, pricing and seat-access rules.

3. Configuring operating modes

The operating modes are configured: autonomous (Offline), online or hybrid with data synchronization.

4. Testing

Financial operations, report generation, QR admission control and overall system stability are tested.

5. Launch and support

After testing, the system goes live. Consultation and further functional expansion are available.

6. Implementation time frame

A basic configuration can be deployed within a few days. More complex seating plans and configurations are handled individually.
