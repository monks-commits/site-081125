Implementation and Support

VA is configured for each venue’s seating plan, ticketing operations and existing commercial workflows.

1. Initial assessment

Confirm the venue layout, seat count, sections, current sales channels, reporting requirements and relationships with existing partners.

2. Digital hall plan

Prepare a venue-specific digital seating plan with pricing zones and seat access rules.

3. Deployment configuration

Set up the applicable online, autonomous or hybrid workflow; integration details are agreed separately.

4. Testing

Test ticket sales, payment boundaries, QR admission, reporting and the reliability of the actual deployment.

5. Launch and service

Move to operation after acceptance testing. VA can be supplied with technical setup and continued support rather than leaving it to the venue’s own IT staff.
