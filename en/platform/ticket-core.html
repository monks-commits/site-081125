VA Ticketing Core

Online sales, box office, ticket office and admission control work within one event and one current seat inventory.

The ticket office is the operational hub

Manage events, seats released for sale, reservations, organizations and authorized distributors, sales, returns and final reporting in one workspace.

Event inventory and ticket turnover

Track released, sold, reserved and returned tickets, available seats, ticket categories and the VA quota. Follow operational changes without losing the history of an event.

Reservations and partners

Keep records of organizations, contacts, seat allocations, reservation deadlines, ticket handover and return operations.

One event, two sales workflows

Online customers move from event poster and Live Poster to the seating plan, payment and electronic ticket. Box-office staff use the same event and inventory to select seats, ticket types and print tickets.

Electronic ticket and admission

Ticket data includes the event, date, seat, price, poster and QR code. At the entrance the scanner confirms a valid admission and identifies a ticket already used. Compensation admission has a separate workflow.

One reporting framework

Online sales, box-office operations, reservations, returns and actual attendance are reflected in the event’s shared ticket accounting and operational reports.
