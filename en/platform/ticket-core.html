Emergency Box-Office Mode

If the Internet connection is temporarily lost, the VA box office continues selling tickets locally. This is especially important just before an event, when queues form at the box office and the cashier cannot reach the server.

To prevent the same seat from being sold simultaneously at the box office and online during an outage, the ticket-office administrator uses a dedicated switch to temporarily disable online sales.

Once the Internet connection returns, local ticket sales are synchronized. After successful synchronization, the administrator re-enables online sales.

Online Sales Switch

The ticket-office administrator disables online sales during the outage and switches them back on after box-office sales are successfully synchronized.

Unified Admission Control

Regardless of the sales channel, tickets are checked by a single admission-control system. The controller does not need to know where a ticket was purchased.

Controller Screen

Standard ticket admission and a separate compensatory-admission mode are both available from one simple interface.

“Admit” / “Already Used”

The first scan confirms the right of admission and records its use. A repeat scan immediately indicates that the same ticket has already been redeemed.

Unified Records and Reporting

Online sales, box-office transactions, reservations, refunds, ticket types and actual attendance are combined in the ticket office's single accounting environment and form shared reporting for each performance.
