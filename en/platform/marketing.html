Marketing and Customer Database

The essentials: what the marketer sees, how the system works with a particular performance, and what the customer database reveals about the audience.

Marketing OS Home Screen

Home-screen cards open the main marketing functions: reviewing actual results, audience research, creating offers, campaigns, planning, execution, performance assessment and renewed audience engagement.

All essential functions on one starting screen

The marketer sees more than disconnected advertising tools: a sequence of working functions connected to actual system data.

Working with an individual performance

After selecting an event, Marketing OS moves from general functions to hands-on work with that specific performance.

“Symphony Evening” — from actual data to results

The workflow shows the status of an individual performance: actuals → analysis → audience → decisions → campaign → plan → execution → results → new knowledge.

Customer Database

The database automatically accumulates actual purchases, tickets and attendance, turning them into useful knowledge about the audience.

Audience segments and activity

Marketing can see the numbers of new, returning, regular, VIP and lapsed customers, and select a group for a particular marketing action.

Customer Profile

Behind the aggregated data is the history of interactions with each individual audience member.

What marketing gains

Purchase and attendance histories, average order value, RFM, status, favourite genres, venue, zone, channel and ticket type help determine whom to approach, with what offer and through which channel — and whom to invite back.
