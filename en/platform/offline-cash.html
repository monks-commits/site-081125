Autonomous Digital Box Office

A local venue ticketing environment for sales, reservations, tickets, admission and reporting without a permanent Internet connection.

A complete local workflow

The autonomous product links the performance, hall plan, seat, reservation, ticket, admission result and accounting inside the venue, rather than acting as an isolated cash register.

Local event editor

Create or adjust performance name, date, time, prices, allocations, status and hall layout in the local system.

Box office and ticket office

The cashier selects a performance, seat and ticket type. The ticket office manages the larger operational flow, including inventory and reservations.

Local QR admission control

The admission device checks tickets against local data and can identify previously used tickets without consulting the cloud for every scan.

Several workplaces, one dataset

A box office, ticket office and entrance control can work in a shared local network with coordinated venue data.

Reports and operational history

Sales, reservations and admission operations are recorded locally and contribute to the venue’s accounting and closing reports.
