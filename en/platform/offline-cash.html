Autonomous Digital Box Office

A complete local ticketing environment without the Internet: sales, tickets, reservations, admission control, records and reporting within the venue's own local system.

Ticketing operations can stay entirely inside the venue

VA Autonomous Digital Box Office is designed for local operation when essential transactions must not depend on continuous access to an external server or the Internet. The ticket office, box office and admission control work together with the venue's shared local data.

More than a cash register — a complete local workflow

The autonomous system covers much more than the sale itself. The links between a performance, seating plan, seat, reservation, ticket, admission check and transaction result are preserved inside the venue.

Performances and hall

Work is organized around actual performances and the venue's real seating plan.

Performance Editor

A performance can be created or edited locally: title, date, time, status, quota, prices and seating plan do not require connection to a cloud service.

Box-Office Sales

The cashier selects a seat and ticket type and completes the sale in the local system.

Reservations

Reservations remain part of unified local records rather than a separate list outside the box-office system.

Tickets

Each sale generates a ticket associated with a specific performance and seat.

Admission Control

The QR code is verified locally. The controller immediately sees the result and whether a ticket has already been used.

Recordkeeping

Transactions are stored within the local system and form a consistent operational history.

Reporting

The box office and ticket office handle not only sales but also the necessary totals and reports.

Box office, ticket office and admission control in one local system

For proper venue operations, different workstations should not maintain separate, incompatible records. The autonomous system supports coordinated work across the local network.

Admission control without Internet access

A ticket's QR code is checked directly within the local VA system. Verification does not require the website, Supabase, a cloud server or the external Internet. The result is generated from the venue's autonomous data.

Camera at the main workstation

A USB camera connects to a computer running the Admission Control module. The controller scans the QR code and the system checks the ticket against the local database.

  • valid ticket;
  • repeat use;
  • missing or unknown ticket.

Separate controller workstation

Admission control can run on a dedicated computer or laptop by the entrance. Workstations are connected through an internal local network, with the camera located at the controller's station.

  • dedicated controller role;
  • local network without external Internet;
  • single ticket-verification database.
Neither setup needs an external Internet connection. The box office, admission control and local database all remain within the venue's network.

Not an “emergency switch” for online sales

The Autonomous Digital Box Office is a separate local system designed for independent operation inside the venue. It should not be confused with a temporary emergency mode of an online system during a brief connection failure.
